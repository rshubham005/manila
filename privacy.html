Privacy policy

Philippines Listing Service ("we", "us") respects your privacy and handles personal information in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect

  • Details you give us in our forms or messages: your name, mobile number, email address, preferred viewing date and time, buyer type and your message.
  • Basic, anonymous website statistics (for example, pages viewed) if website analytics are switched on.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, arrange and confirm viewings, and send you the property information you asked for.
  • To let you know about future listings, only if you've asked us to.

Our forms open WhatsApp or your email app so you can review your message before sending it. We don't sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to handle your enquiry or a resulting transaction, or as required by law.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Contact us at hello@philippineslistingservice.com or 0962 448 8936.

Property disclaimer

Renovation concept images

Most images in our property galleries are digital renovation concepts. They show one possible way the spaces could look after renovation and are provided for illustration only. Furniture, décor, finishes, fixtures, landscaping and architectural changes shown in these concepts are not included in the sale and do not represent the property's present condition. Each concept includes an inset photo of the room as it is today, and our "As it is today" section shows the actual current condition of each space.

Sold in its current condition

The Bel-Air II property is an existing home offered in its current, as-is condition. Buyers are encouraged to inspect the property in person and to carry out their own due diligence, including title verification and technical inspections.

Listing information

All property details, measurements, prices and terms are provided in good faith and are subject to verification, change, prior sale or withdrawal without notice. Information on this website does not form part of any offer or contract. Redevelopment potential is subject to applicable laws, village rules and permits.

Distances and travel times

Distances and travel times to nearby places are approximate and depend on traffic and route.

Who can buy land in the Philippines

Under the Philippine Constitution, land is generally reserved for Filipino citizens and qualified Philippine corporations. Former natural-born Filipinos and dual citizens may be eligible under specific laws. This is general information, not legal advice — please consult a qualified lawyer about your own situation before making an offer.

Nothing on this website is financial, tax or legal advice. Buyers should obtain independent professional advice.
