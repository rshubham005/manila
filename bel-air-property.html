The residence

Own a rare property in one of Makati's most prestigious gated communities — perfect for growing families, investors and developers looking to renovate, modernise or redevelop a premium address.

The property combines a main house and a separate guest house on a single 502 sqm east-facing end lot — two homes with their own water and electric utilities, a private swimming pool, spacious and flexible living areas, and parking for up to eleven cars. It is an excellent redevelopment opportunity, with potential to build two 3-storey homes, subject to applicable regulations.

Property note. This is an existing home being offered in its current condition, ideal for buyers looking to renovate, modernise or redevelop while securing a premium address in Bel-Air II, Makati. Gallery images are renovation concepts; see As it stands for the current condition.
